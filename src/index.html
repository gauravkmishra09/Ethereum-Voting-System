Your browser handles the preview. This page does not upload or store camera footage. A real deployment must use an approved identity/eligibility provider; Aadhaar/DigiLocker must never be simulated as verified.

Before submitting

1. Install or unlock MetaMask in your browser.
2. Switch to Sepolia and ensure you have test ETH for gas.
3. Review your selected candidate.
4. Confirm the transaction in MetaMask and wait for the blockchain receipt.

The smart contract currently enforces one vote per wallet address. That is not the same as one vote per eligible person.
